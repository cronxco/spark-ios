Apple rebuilt Siri on Foundation Models

AppleSiriSpark iOS
Summary

Apple has replaced the intent-matching core of Siri with an on-device Foundation Model, escalating to Private Cloud Compute only when a request exceeds what the phone can answer.

For four years the interesting question about Siri was not what it could do but why it could not do more. The answer was always the same: the intent graph was hand-built, and hand-built graphs do not generalise.

Key Takeaways
App Intents carry semantic indexing through IndexedEntity.
Third-party apps expose entities, not raw intents.
On-device handles summarisation; PCC handles reasoning.
